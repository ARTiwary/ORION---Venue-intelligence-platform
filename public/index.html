How it works

One assistant, six specialties

Every question — from a fan, a volunteer, or a steward in the control room — is understood and routed automatically to the right specialist, then answered as a single, coherent voice.

Wayfinding

Personalized routes through gates, concourses and seating, generated for the fan in front of you.

Crowd flow

Short-horizon congestion forecasts, written in plain language before a bottleneck forms.

Languages

Natural, culturally-aware answers in dozens of languages — not literal translation.

Accessibility

Step-free routes and sensory guidance tailored to each visitor's needs.

Transport

Last-mile planning and dispersal guidance that keeps egress calm and fast.

Sustainability

A live footprint reading for every journey, with a nudge toward the lighter option.
